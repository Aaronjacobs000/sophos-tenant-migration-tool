Job details

A started migration can't be cancelled. The Sophos migrations API has no cancel or delete, so the move runs to the end. A receiving job that the sending tenant never picks up stays listed until it expires, 14 days after it was created.

Group membership

Adds each moved device to a destination group, so policies assigned to that group follow it. Each device starts on the group with the same name as its group on the sending tenant. Pick another group, or Don't add, in its row; a device with no such group waits for you to pick one. Devices already in the group are skipped. To use the source's group, mirror it on the Groups page first.

Available once devices have moved.

Scan arrived devices

Asks each device that has checked in to run a scan under the receiving tenant's policies. Sophos only says a scan was requested; one that runs shows as an event on the device in Sophos Fusion. Many devices scanning at once slows them down, servers especially.

Available once devices have checked in.

Tamper protection

A moved device takes the receiving tenant's tamper protection, and the move already gives it a new password from that tenant. This turns tamper protection on for devices that have checked in. Make new passwords changes it again, for example if you shared it during the move. It can't turn tamper protection on while it is off for the whole tenant. These choices are remembered for this job in this browser.

Available once devices have checked in.
